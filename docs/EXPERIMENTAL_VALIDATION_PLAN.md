# Experimental validation plan

## Priority set

Begin with the nine families unresolved by both CDD and the curated structural reference set. Include two catalogued families with well-supported annotations as positive process controls and nArcR-20/nArcR-24 as computationally weak motif controls.

## Staged design

1. Express and purify one representative protein per family; verify monodispersity and oligomeric state.
2. Test binding to the predicted operator and sequence-scrambled controls by EMSA across a concentration series. Estimate apparent affinity only where a saturable shift is observed.
3. Confirm binding and specificity with an orthogonal quantitative assay such as fluorescence anisotropy, microscale thermophoresis, or surface plasmon resonance.
4. Mutate conserved motif bases and predicted DNA-contact residues. Prespecify the expected loss-of-binding direction.
5. Perturb the native gene by deletion, depletion, or CRISPR interference and perform replicated RNA sequencing with an appropriate time course and controls.
6. Combine differential expression with ChIP-seq/CUT&Tag or equivalent occupancy evidence to distinguish direct from indirect targets.

## Minimum reporting

Report biological and technical replicate counts, randomization/blinding where applicable, uncropped gels, concentration ranges, exclusion criteria, effect sizes with intervals, full oligonucleotide sequences, expression constructs, raw instrument files, and analysis code. A family should be called a transcriptional regulator only when direct DNA binding and a compatible in vivo regulatory effect are both supported.
